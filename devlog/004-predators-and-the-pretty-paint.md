DevLog #4 predators and the pretty paint

Predators are in. Aggression is now a spectrum: every creature hunts or
grazes with probability equal to its aggression, and you only catch prey
you can actually outrun. so prey evolve to run faster, predators evolve
to keep up, and both drift up in the sparklines. it took way longer than
the food system - first version collapsed the whole population into
hyper-carnivores. also everything glows now, green to amber to red as
they get dangerous.
